My story

I’m a student exploring how technology can make information clearer and everyday tasks easier. I enjoy moving from a rough question to a small working prototype, then improving it through feedback.

Right now, I’m focused on strengthening the fundamentals: writing readable code, designing for different people and devices, and explaining the decisions behind my work.

How I approach projects

  1. Understand the problem. Write down who the work is for and what success should look like.
  2. Start small. Build the simplest version that can test the main idea.
  3. Ask for feedback. Listen for confusion, friction, and opportunities to simplify.
  4. Document the learning. Record what worked, what changed, and what I would try next.
